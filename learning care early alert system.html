使用者知情同意說明

歡迎使用「安安」AI 學習陪伴服務。開始使用前，請先閱讀以下說明。當您勾選同意並開始使用本服務，即表示您已了解本服務的角色、資料使用目的與使用限制，並同意以下內容。

Welcome to Anan, the AI learning companion service. Before using this service, please read the following information. By checking the consent box and starting the service, you acknowledge that you understand the role of this service, the purpose of data use, and its limitations.

一、安安的服務角色

（一）安安是國立東華大學學習關懷預警系統中的 AI 學習陪伴，主要協助學生進行學習相關的整理與規劃，例如：課業重點整理、作業與報告準備、考試準備、跨域修課思考、時間管理、學習策略建議，以及自我學習狀況的初步梳理等。

（二）安安的目的是提供溫和引導與具體建議，協助您更清楚地面對學習問題，並思考可行的下一步行動。

(1) Anan is the AI learning companion in the National Dong Hwa University Learning Care Early Alert System. It helps students organize and plan learning-related matters such as course review, assignments and reports, exam preparation, interdisciplinary learning, time management, learning strategies, and initial reflection on learning conditions.

(2) Anan provides gentle guidance and concrete suggestions to help you understand your learning concerns more clearly and consider feasible next steps.

二、資料使用目的

您在本服務中輸入的訊息，會用於產生當次 AI 回覆，協助安安理解您的學習需求、問題脈絡與希望獲得的協助；學生基本資料、逐筆對話、AI 回覆、情境標籤、關懷層級與系統時間也會寫入學校設定的試算表，作為學習關懷紀錄。

本系統紀錄僅供系統管理者及學校授權的學習關懷相關人員，依學習支持、系統維護與必要協助目的查看；不作為正式課業評量、學籍處分或公開展示使用。

本服務使用資料的目的包括：

  1. 回應您當次提出的學習問題。
  2. 協助整理您提供的學習資訊與需求。
  3. 提供與課業安排、學習策略、時間規劃或修課思考有關的建議。
  4. 維持短期對話脈絡，使回覆較為連貫；每位學生每日以學號計算最多 20 輪對話。

Messages you enter are used to generate the current AI response and help Anan understand your learning needs and context. Basic student information, each student message, AI responses, scenario tags, care level, and system time may also be written to the school-designated spreadsheet as a learning care record.

These records are available only to system administrators and school-authorized learning care personnel for learning support, system maintenance, and necessary assistance. They are not used as formal academic evaluation, student-status decisions, or public display.

The purposes of data use include: responding to your current learning question; organizing the learning information and needs you provide; offering suggestions about coursework, learning strategies, time planning, or course decisions; and maintaining short-term context so responses are more coherent. Each student may have up to 20 conversation turns per day based on student ID.

三、使用提醒

（一）安安提供的是學習陪伴與一般性建議，並非正式課業評量、學籍判定、行政決定或心理諮商診斷的依據。

（二）若涉及成績、選課、畢業門檻、學籍、請假、休退學、獎助學金或其他正式行政事項，請以課程教師、系所辦公室、學校相關單位或正式公告為準。

（三）若您的情況涉及急迫危機、強烈情緒困擾、自傷或傷人風險，或需要真人即時協助，請立即聯絡導師、系所辦公室、學務處、諮商輔導中心、校安中心或當地緊急求助管道。安安無法取代即時危機處理或專業諮商服務。

(1) Anan provides learning companionship and general suggestions. It is not a basis for formal academic assessment, student-status decisions, administrative decisions, or psychological counseling diagnosis.

(2) For grades, course selection, graduation requirements, student status, leave, withdrawal or suspension, scholarships, grants, or other official administrative matters, please refer to course instructors, department offices, relevant school units, or official announcements.

(3) If your situation involves urgent crisis, intense emotional distress, risk of self-harm or harm to others, or the need for immediate human assistance, please contact your advisor, department office, Office of Student Affairs, Counseling Center, Campus Security Center, or local emergency support. Anan cannot replace immediate crisis response or professional counseling services.

四、您的同意

您勾選同意並開始使用本服務，即表示您已了解並同意：

  1. 本服務可依據您輸入的訊息產生 AI 回覆。
  2. 您在本服務中輸入的訊息與系統產生的標籤，將用於當次 AI 回覆、短期對話脈絡與學習關懷紀錄。
  3. 安安提供的是學習陪伴與一般性建議，不取代教師、導師、諮商心理師、校內行政單位或其他專業人員的正式協助。
  4. 若涉及正式課業、行政、心理諮商或緊急協助事項，您應尋求校內外適當專業資源。

By checking consent and starting this service, you agree that the service may generate AI responses based on your input; your messages and system-generated tags may be used for the current AI response, short-term conversation context, and learning care records; Anan does not replace formal assistance from teachers, advisors, counseling psychologists, school administrative units, or other professionals; and you should seek appropriate professional resources for formal academic, administrative, counseling, or emergency matters.

國立東華大學學習關懷預警系統

安安是國立東華大學學習關懷預警系統的 AI 學習夥伴。你可以把課程進度、作業、考試準備、跨域修課或時間安排等學習上的困難或卡點，用自己的話說出來；安安會先試圖理解情況，再協助你釐清與改善你遇到的學習問題。

學生註冊

第一次使用請建立學生帳號，安安會用這些資訊理解你的學院、系所與學習脈絡；之後請用同一個 email 登入。

同學您好，您的問題可以進一步洽詢學校導師、行政人員或心理諮商中心，相信可以獲得更好的協助。
